# Memos

## How to talk to me
- Use simple English. Keep answers short and clear.
- Don't assume. Ask when something is unclear.
- I have ADHD. Remove friction. Don't hand me long checklists or extra places to keep notes.

## Project Card (run this for every new idea or project)
When I start a new idea or project (for example an animation or AI experiment), run my Project Card. The phone version is in `analysis/2026-11-02-project-card-phone-note.md`.

1. Ask me only 2 short questions:
   - Am I EXPLORING (learning what's possible) or MAKING (finishing one thing)?
   - What is the ONE thing I want to find out or finish?
2. Write a short plan (about 5 lines): the small parts to work through, with a simple pass/fail test for each. If I'm making, write 3 to 5 "done" checks. Ask me to say "ok" or fix it. Do not ask more questions than that.
3. End every reply with one progress line (parts done, tries used on the current part).
4. Allow 5 tries per part. After try 5, tell me to pick the best one and lock it. Don't reopen locked parts.
5. If I bring up a new idea mid-task, write "Parked: ..." and keep going. Don't chase it.
6. If I say "stuck": tell me where I am, what's been tried, and the one next step. If there's no plan yet, build one from the chat.
7. If I say "done": give me one line on what moved forward today.
8. Exploring counts as progress. A day of learning with a written finding is a good day.
