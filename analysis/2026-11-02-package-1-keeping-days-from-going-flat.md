KEEPING MY DAYS FROM GOING FLAT
(Draft, 2 Nov 2026. Pin this.)

WHY I'M DOING THIS
When I stay on my phone doing one thing (like AI projects) all day, I feel useless by evening. On days I mix it up (walk, chores, cooking, puzzle, time with Serene or my husband), I feel better. This is a good bet, not proven. I will test it for 2 weeks.

----------------
START HERE (no alarm needed)

THE SWITCH
Switching = doing something ELSE for 30 minutes. It is not doing nothing. It must be OFF my phone.

WHEN TO SWITCH (my 2 signals)
1) When the AI is generating something slow. Start it, then go switch until it's ready.
2) When I finish a project part, or hit 5 tries on it. Pick my best one, then switch for 30 minutes.

Then pick ONE thing from the SWITCH MENU below.

If I skip it: no guilt. Do it at the next signal.

----------------
MUST (every day, small)
- Walk
- Faith: learn 5-10 minutes, then write one sentence: "Today I will do ___ because of what I learned."
- Dinner: ask one real question

----------------
SWITCH MENU (pick one)
- Clean or organize one small thing
- Cook or prep food
- Puzzle for 15-30 minutes
- One-on-one time with Serene
- Talk with my husband
- Garden or other physical activity
- Strength session (Tuesday and Thursday)
- Text a friend (1st = friend #1, 10th = friend #2, 20th = Anna)
- Step outside for a few minutes
(Add anything I like.)

ACTIVE REST (I can't do "nothing", and that's OK)
Puzzle, walk with a podcast, cooking. These count as rest.

----------------
WHEN I START A NEW IDEA (Project Card)
1) Am I EXPLORING (learning what's possible) or MAKING (finishing ONE thing)?
2) What is the ONE thing I want to find out or finish?
Then: break it into small parts. 5 tries max per part, then pick the best and move on. New idea pops up? Write "PARK: ..." and keep going.
Making? Write 3-5 checks for "done" first. No adding checks later.
Works for work projects too, like the Business Hub: ship the simplest v1 first.

----------------
END OF DAY (1 minute)
Write one line: "Today I finished / learned ______."
Mood 1-5. Tick what I did: moved my body / was with people / finished something I can see.

----------------
SUNDAY CHECK (5 minutes)
Rate the 8 areas (health, family, friends, faith, fun/creative, work, money, home). Did each get its small minimum this week? Yes or no. If no, make it smaller. Don't try harder.

----------------
READING (optional. Only when I feel like it.)
- Puttylike (Emilie Wapnick): website, community, book "How to Be Everything"
- "Refuse to Choose!" (Barbara Sher): for people with many interests. Take the ideas; skip the notebook if I don't want one.
- Ikigai: the original idea is meaning in small daily things, not one big purpose. (Mieko Kamiya; Ken Mogi, "The Little Book of Ikigai")
I have not read these books. Check them first.

----------------
2-WEEK TEST
Am I feeling better at the end of the day when I follow this? If yes, keep it. If no, change it.

----------------
KNOWN GAP
My two signals only cover project work. If I'm just scrolling or consuming on my phone, nothing tells me to switch. Watch for that.
