## How I keep all areas moving (the 3 rules)
1. Attach it to something that already happens (dinner, the walk, school runs).
2. Involve another person or a fixed time.
3. Use a tiny weekly minimum. Goal is "did it get its minimum?", not perfect.

## Faith routine (learn one, apply one)
1. **Learn:** one small piece from one source I trust.
2. **Apply:** write one sentence, "Today I will do ___ because of what I learned."
3. **Night check:** did I do it? Yes / no.
4. **Sunday:** pick the one thing that stuck and carry it forward.
For religious questions, ask a teacher or imam I trust.

## Friends plan (text-friendly, no calls needed)
- **Anna:** keep the meet-up every ~2 months. Add one text a month in between.
- **Out-of-state friends:** text on a fixed day each month (for example the 1st). A voice note is fine.
- **Optional idea:** a local masjid class or study circle for faith and friends together.

## Sunday check (5 minutes)
For each area: did it get its minimum? Yes or no.
- If no: do not try harder. Move it to a time that already exists.
- If no twice in a row: make it smaller.
