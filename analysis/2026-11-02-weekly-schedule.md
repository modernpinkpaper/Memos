# My Weekly Schedule (life areas)

Draft, 2 Nov 2026. Built on the weekly page (`2026-11-02-life-areas-weekly-page.md`).
I don't know your exact clock times, so each item is tied to something that already happens ("anchors"). Change any anchor.
Skeleton for creative experiments is ON HOLD.

Extra time needed: about 15-20 minutes on most days.

## Every day

| When | Do this | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Morning, with coffee (or after a prayer, if that suits you) | Learn 5-10 min from your one faith source. Write ONE sentence: "Today I will do ___ because of what I learned." | Faith |
| Walk (most days) | Walk. Listen to anything you like. | Health |
| Dinner | Ask one real question at the table (not "how was school"). | Family |
| Night, 1 minute | Did I do my faith sentence? Yes / no. That's all. | Faith |

## Day by day

| Day | Extra things (on top of "every day") |
|---|---|
| **Monday** | Pick ONE small thing to ship at work this week and write it down. |
| **Tuesday** | Strength session (10-15 min) while dinner cooks. Save a favorite show/audiobook for this only. |
| **Wednesday** | Midweek faith check (2 min): which of my sentences did I actually do? Pick one to repeat. Creative block (30-45 min). |
| **Thursday** | Strength session (10-15 min) while dinner cooks. |
| **Friday** | Check the work ship: done or not? (Optional: Jumu'ah if you attend. Tell me if you want it on the schedule.) |
| **Saturday** | One planned family thing (a walk, a meal out, a game, anything small). Creative block (30-45 min). |
| **Sunday** | 5-minute check (see below). Prep dinner for the week if it helps (like the chicken). |

## Monthly (set repeating phone reminders)

| Date | Do this | Area |
|---|---|---|
| 1st of month | Text friend #1 (out of state). Voice note is fine. | Friends |
| 10th of month | Text friend #2 (out of state). | Friends |
| 20th of month | Text Anna (a photo or "thinking of you"). | Friends |
| Every ~2 months | Meet Anna. Put the next date in the calendar when you leave. | Friends |

## Sunday 5-minute check
For each area, did it get its minimum this week? Yes or no.
- Health: walks + 2 strength sessions
- Family: one planned thing + dinner questions
- Friends: this month's text(s) sent
- Faith: at least 4 days of learn + apply
- Fun/creative: 2 blocks
- Work: the one shipped thing
If no, don't try harder. Move it to a time that already exists. If no twice in a row, make it smaller.

Money (10) and Home (8) need nothing extra.
